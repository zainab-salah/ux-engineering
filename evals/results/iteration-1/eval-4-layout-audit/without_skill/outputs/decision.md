## Route and screen-size coverage

Apply shell, control, focus and spacing changes in shared styles so inner pages inherit them. Directory-specific changes are the toolbar, table, pagination and activity area. No inner-page source was supplied, so their current appearance and behavior have not been audited.

- On wide screens, let the table use available width without stretching short controls unnecessarily. Keep the main action near the title or filters.
- On short laptop screens and at increased text size, let `main` scroll through all content while the header remains available. Avoid fixed content heights that clip forms or put pagination out of reach.
- On narrow screens, wrap or stack filters, the main action and pagination without changing their reading order. Long client names must wrap or remain accessible within the table region; they must not widen the whole shell.
- On client detail/edit pages, preserve a clear route title, back navigation and labeled fields. Long forms, validation messages and action groups must fit the same scrolling shell. Do not force a directory table height onto those routes.
- Treat loading, no search matches, empty activity, errors and populated results as distinct states. Use concise messages and relevant recovery actions without reserving large blank panels. Do not erase filter input when a request fails.

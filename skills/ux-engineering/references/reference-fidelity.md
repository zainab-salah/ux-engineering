# Supplied references and responsive composition

Use this guidance when the integration or refinement has an explicit visual reference. It supplements the backend-to-interaction workflow; it does not trigger an unrelated redesign.

## Establish what must match

- Identify the approved reference, supplied assets and latest constraints. Separate fixed composition/content from areas open to interpretation. Follow the current asset policy when it supersedes earlier permission.
- Reproduce the static hierarchy, relative proportions, alignment anchors, content order and visual weight before adding motion. Do not relocate a key element because another arrangement is easier to animate.
- Compare the actual rendered result with the reference at comparable dimensions. State mismatches and unverified sizes; matching colors alone does not establish fidelity.

## Adapt relationships across screen sizes

Preserve meaningful relationships, such as a heading aligned with its related content, rather than copying desktop pixel positions into every viewport. Establish what stacks, wraps or moves on a narrow screen. Keep readable text, useful visuals, safe interior padding and reachable actions without excessive blank space. Dense two-dimensional data may scroll within its region instead of shrinking until unreadable.

Use approved assets instead of unrequested substitutes. Preserve useful aspect ratios and inspect cropping, transparency and overflow at each relevant size. Keep meaningful text in HTML, supply appropriate image alternatives, and keep decorative layers out of interaction/focus paths.

Keep related sections consistent through shared layout, typography and component rules while preserving their navigation and semantic structure. Derive those rules from the current product or approved reference; do not carry a previous project's aesthetic into the next project.

## Add purposeful motion after the layout works

Specify the trigger, feedback and settled state. Motion should communicate an interaction or supported state without changing the approved layout, blocking content or adding unnecessary work. Reuse existing capabilities before adding a library.

Content and actions remain available without hover, scroll animation or motion. Honor reduced motion, scope keyboard shortcuts to the relevant interactive area, avoid clipping focus or animated content, and verify touch/keyboard behavior.

## Keep evidence truthful

Use real approved product screenshots and verified content. For private products, use an authorized sanitized diagram or permitted description rather than inventing screens, capabilities or numbers. Do not copy private data into a public showcase. Record browser and performance checks actually performed; a successful build does not prove visual fidelity.
